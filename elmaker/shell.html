??What this tool will not let you do

There are no colour pickers and no font controls. The palette, the type and the layout are fixed, so a graphic made here cannot come out off brand.

One idea per graphic. One top line, one bottom line. A second point is a second graphic.

The mark sits in the white bar and nothing else goes in there.

Gold carries dark text, never white. That is why the gold band flips the text colour.

A card may not characterise a report or draw a conclusion from one. Describe what someone saw. Never why it happened, never who did it.

Words that do not go on a graphic: defend, fight, resist, stolen, rigged, crisis.
